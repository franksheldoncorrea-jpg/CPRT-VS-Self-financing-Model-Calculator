Modeling assumptions⌄
  • Depreciation is straight-line over useful life; stops once the asset is fully depreciated.
  • Loan principal is repaid in equal instalments over the loan tenor; interest is charged on the reducing balance.
  • Tax is applied directly to EBT each year, including as a shield on losses — this assumes the diagnostics unit's results are pooled with a profitable parent company (standard for evaluating a business unit).
  • Patient-facing price and volume are assumed identical whether the equipment is owned or CPRT-financed, so revenue nets out of the breakeven rate — only costs differentiate the two models.
  • Salvage value (if any) is realized at the end of the evaluation period and taxed on the gain over remaining book value.
  • The CPRT model carries no depreciation, debt, or upfront capex — the per-test rate is assumed to bundle the vendor's own cost recovery.
  • All cash flows are discounted at the WACC / hurdle rate you enter.
